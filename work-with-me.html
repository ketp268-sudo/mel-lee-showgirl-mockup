Good to know

Questions, answered.

Do I need to be at a certain level?+

No. Every session and feedback review is tailored to where you are, from first performance to seasoned pro.

Where are you based?+

Everywhere and nowhere — I tour. Online sessions run on Zoom from anywhere in the world; in-person sessions happen across the UK and in whichever city I'm performing or teaching.

How does Performance Feedback work?+

Buy, upload your video via the link in your receipt, get a recorded review within five working days.

Why the Signature Series?+

Four sessions let us build something — one session fixes a problem, four change how you perform. It includes a recorded video review between sessions and it is what most of my clients choose.

Can I reschedule?+

Once, with 72 hours' notice. Later changes count as a new booking. All sales are final.

Ready to own the stage?

Pick the level of support you want — a one-off session, the four-session Series, or a recorded review of your routine. Online worldwide, in person across the UK.
