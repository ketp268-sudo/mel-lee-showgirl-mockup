Good to know

Questions, answered.

Anything else? Send Mel a message — replies within two working days.

Do I need to be at a certain level?+

No. Every session and feedback review is tailored to where you are, from first performance to seasoned pro.

Where are you based?+

Everywhere and nowhere — I tour. Online sessions run on Zoom from anywhere in the world; in-person sessions happen across the UK and in whichever city I'm performing or teaching. Follow @melee.x for where I am next.

Online or in person?+

Both. Online sessions run on Zoom; in-person pole sessions add studio hire at cost.

What happens after I book?+

You'll get an email from me within two working days to schedule and talk through your goals.

Why the Signature Series?+

Four sessions let us build something — one session fixes a problem, four change how you perform. It includes a recorded video review between sessions and it is what most of my clients choose.

Can I reschedule?+

Once, with 72 hours' notice. Later changes count as a new booking. All sales are final.

I run a studio — can Mel teach for us?+

Yes: see Workshops. From £160 per 75-minute workshop; minimum two workshops for international bookings.
